PrivAI Enterprise Technology Policies
Version 2026.1 - Confidential Internal Guideline

1. General Principles
All employees and contractors must adhere to data minimization principles when interacting with AI systems.
Direct transmission of unredacted personal identifiers to external cloud providers is strictly prohibited.

2. Access Controls
Access to internal databases requires authenticated multi-factor credentials and authorized role permits.
System administrators must periodically review access logs and verify checkpoint consistency.

3. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security deviation or policy bypass attempt must be reported immediately to the Information Security team.
Standard response times: High severity within 1 hour; Medium severity within 4 hours.
